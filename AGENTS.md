# MiaoYan Website Agent Guide

This is the website checkout on the `vercel` branch. The app source and its Xcode commands live on a different branch.

- `index.html`, `privacy.html`, `assets/`, and `data/` contain site content; `vercel.json` owns redirects.
- `scripts/build-site.py` generates the home pages from one copy table per language: `/` (English, the default) plus `/zh/`, `/tw/`, `/ja/`, `/ko/`, `/de/`, `/fr/` and `/es/`, the README's seven languages and the app's Spanish. Edit it and run `python3 -I scripts/build-site.py` instead of editing the generated `index.html` files; a copy change lands in every language in the same commit. The pages use `site.css`, which both generators inline into every page's head (rerun them after editing it), whose type and tokens follow mole.fit (one serif face: the Source Han subset ahead of Charter for Chinese, Mincho for Japanese; Korean is the system sans with a serif wordmark, since iOS has no Myeongjo). The hero wordmark is near-black `MiaoYan 語` in every language, the family's one Traditional character per product (Mole 鼴, Kaku 書, Pake 殼, Hina 賞): 語 comes from the one-glyph `fonts/miaoyan-mark.woff2` that `scripts/build-fonts.py` cuts from Source Han Serif TC, with one small 荷叶绿 dot tracing a figure eight across it, counter-clockwise over 16s, with no drawn path. Near-black is 长石灰 `#363433` (2026-10-19), and the nav brand `MiaoYan` uses it rather than the green. Pages other than `/zh/` and `/tw/` show the English screenshots. The footer is mole.fit's dark band in MiaoYan's `--night`, as on the other product sites (maintainer 2026-10-18, after an ivory round): `MiaoYan · 語`, one about line, then Get / Help / Connect columns, with Mole's text steps. `privacy.html` and `404.html` are hand-written, but `build-site.py` rewrites their nav, footer and nav script from the English copy on every run (2026-10-19: both had shipped with a bare brand-only bar), so edit their body only.
- The blog lives at `/blog/` (English) and `/zh/blog/` (Simplified Chinese). Sources are `content/blog/index.json` (slug, date, modified, category, en and zh title and description) plus `content/blog/<slug>.en.md` and `<slug>.zh.md`; `python3 -I scripts/build-blog.py` writes `blog/`, `zh/blog/` (index, `<slug>/index.html`, `feed.xml`) and the marked blog blocks of `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt`, taking the header, footer and scripts from `scripts/build-site.py`. Run it after `build-site.py`, then `python3 scripts/build-fonts.py` when a Chinese post changes, since the SC subset covers `zh/blog/` too; `build-blog.py --check` fails on stale output, an unknown category, a missing figure, a broken internal link or a download link. Categories are `writing` (pillar `markdown-editor-for-mac`) and `notes` (pillar `local-first-markdown-notes`); a new one goes into `CATEGORIES` and `CATEGORY_COPY` first. A post's cover is `img/blog/cards/<slug>.webp` (1600x900) and appears only once the file exists; `[[figure:<name>|<alt>|<caption>]]` embeds `img/blog/<name>.webp`, saved at twice its display width (the article column fills the page since 2026-10-21, as on every product site, and was 720px before). An h2 `FAQ` / `常见问题` with h3 questions becomes FAQPage data. Posts follow the site rule below: App Store and GitHub links only, never a Mac download.
- Chinese fonts (maintainer 2026-11-08): Simplified reading text is Clear Han Serif, titles and Traditional stay Source Han Serif, all `font-display: block` with no `local()` and preloaded at high priority. After `python3 -I scripts/build-fonts.py` run it again with `--stamp`, which writes the `?v=` content hashes into `site.css`, then `build-site.py` and `build-blog.py`; `--check` fails on a stale hash or a non-blocking face.
- `2ca82b8937b6fed95beebabb5361a058.txt` at the root is the IndexNow key (Bing, Yandex and others), added 2026-11-06; keep it, or IndexNow submissions for miaoyan.app stop being accepted. After a batch of new or changed pages, submit their URLs once to `https://api.indexnow.org/indexnow` with that key; it is a change notice, not an indexing guarantee. `BingSiteAuth.xml` beside it verifies the site in Bing Webmaster Tools (tw93's account, 2026-11-06); keep it too.
- The site recommends the App Store and links only to the App Store and the GitHub repository, never to a Mac download (maintainer, 2026-10-11). Store links carry `data-store`: iPhone and iPad get the region-less link, which opens the App Store app, the Mac gets `macappstore://`, and other devices keep the default, `/cn/` on the Chinese page because a desktop browser in China loses the app on the region-less link.
- The devices section has one App Store QR code beside its store button (`assets/qr-app-store.svg`, maintainer 2026-10-27): the region-less link, error correction H with the bird in a cleared centre, shown at 74px so each of its 37 modules is 2px. It is hidden on touch devices and at 560px and below, where visitors tap the button instead. A new app id means regenerating it (segno) and decoding the rendered file at 1x and 2x before shipping.
- `cats.html` now redirects to cats.tw93.fun (`vercel.json`) and its files are gone. `tailwind.css` and `build.css` stay only for the sponsors pipeline in `docs/sponsors-board.md`; no page loads them.
- `.vercelignore` keeps `AGENTS.md`, `docs/`, `data/` and `scripts/` out of the deployment, so they are not served. A new internal file goes there too.
- Google Analytics runs in consent mode with storage denied, so the site sets no cookies and needs no consent banner; `privacy.html` says so. Keep the two in step. gtag.js loads on idle after the load event, and a page prerendered on hover waits for activation; every page carries speculation rules that prerender internal links on hover, minus the language menu, because the English home redirects by the remembered language before the click stores the new one.
- `/payroom` is the maintainer's private sales page (`api/payroom.js`, password from `MIAOYAN_PAYROOM_PASSWORD`; never link it from a public page). `api/payroom-sync.js` runs on a cron every three hours and writes one snapshot to the private Blob store `miaoyan-payroom`; the page reads only that snapshot and never calls Apple or Google on a view. Sales, refunds, finance, review status, reviews and the Apple channel reports come from App Store Connect (`ASC_*`), and site traffic from GA4 through the read-only service account `MIAOYAN_GA_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON`. A failed part keeps its last good data and says so at the top of the page; a failed Blob read stops the sync instead of writing an empty store. In a Sales report a refund has negative Units and Customer Price but a positive Developer Proceeds, so proceeds are always units times the column. Apple reports aggregates only: there are no orders, buyers or refund reasons to show. `node scripts/test-payroom.mjs` holds the numbers.
- `appcast.xml` and `Release/` are distribution surfaces. Changes need the actual published version, download, and artifact verified; do not infer release success from XML syntax alone.
- `npm run sponsors` invokes a network-backed generator, not a read-only verifier. Read its inputs and output paths before running it.
- Check local links for documentation edits and render affected pages for visual changes. Preserve unrelated site work and stable asset URLs.
