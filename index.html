为什么选择妙言Why MiaoYan

本地Local-first

Markdown 文件保存在你选择的本地文件夹，不收集笔记数据。需要跨设备时，可交给 iCloud Drive、坚果云或你熟悉的文件同步工具。Your Markdown files stay in a folder you choose. MiaoYan does not collect note data; iCloud Drive, Nutstore, or another file-sync client can handle cross-device sync.

专注Focused

文件夹、笔记列表与编辑器组成清楚的三栏布局。支持深色模式、无干扰写作和常用快捷键，不需要维护插件系统。A clear three-column layout keeps folders, notes, and writing together. Dark mode, distraction-free writing, and keyboard shortcuts are built in without a plugin system to maintain.

原生Native

使用 Swift 6 原生开发，不依赖 Electron。分栏模式提供实时预览与 60fps 双向滚动同步，适合专注处理纯 Markdown。Built natively with Swift 6 instead of Electron. Split mode pairs real-time preview with 60fps bidirectional scroll sync for focused Markdown editing.

够用Capable

支持双向链接与反向链接、LaTeX、Mermaid、版本历史、自动排版和命令行操作，也能用 PPT 模式演示 Markdown。Wikilinks and backlinks, LaTeX, Mermaid, version history, auto-formatting, a CLI, and Markdown presentations cover the work around writing.

妙言适合谁Who MiaoYan is for

和 Typora 相比Compared with Typora

妙言不做所见即所得编辑，而是保留纯 Markdown 编辑与独立预览。它更适合希望直接掌控源文件，又需要即时预览的人。MiaoYan keeps pure Markdown editing and a separate preview instead of WYSIWYG. It fits people who want direct control of source files with immediate visual feedback.

和 Obsidian 相比Compared with Obsidian

妙言没有 Electron 运行时，也不以插件生态为中心。它更适合想要原生、轻量、本地文件和少量内置能力的人。MiaoYan has no Electron runtime and is not centered on a plugin ecosystem. It fits people who prefer a native, lightweight app with local files and focused built-in features.
