Release Notes

What changed in each version of MiaoYan, newest first.

V4.8.0 Brachydios 2026-11-06

  1. Deleted notes go to the library's own Trash, so iPhone, iPad and Mac share one Trash, and emptying it on iPhone removes the Mac's Trash row too

Mac

  1. When the disk is full or a folder cannot be written, a failed save is reported once instead of over and over while you type, and Command-S tells you when it still cannot save
  2. Deleting a note shows Moved to Trash with an Undo button, an empty folder or a search with no match shows a short hint instead of a blank editor, and toasts stay long enough to read and while the pointer is over them
  3. Formulas typeset correctly offline, and mind maps draw their branches in MiaoYan's own colours and no longer come out blank in PDF and image exports
  4. Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Spanish menus and messages use native wording, English note counts read 1 note and 2 notes, English and Spanish word counts count words, and version history dates follow the app language
  5. On first launch the sample folders are named in the system language, and the sample notes write shortcuts as the menus show them
  6. Folder names in the sidebar line up with their icons, the MiaoYan logo sits further below the window buttons, interface greys are warmer, note list dates are larger, other find matches sit on a light green tint, and the find bar's shadow now shows

iPhone and iPad

  1. No iCloud account needed to start: MiaoYan makes a library on this device, and you can see it in the Files app
  2. A note that can't be opened says why in one line, with a Try again button
  3. A folder's card counts the same notes its page lists, and without iCloud the header reads iCloud Off instead of Synced
  4. The Edit button is a plain pencil, and the launch screen uses the same paper as the app

V4.7.1 Rathalos 2026-11-06

Mac

  1. Images and files dragged from Finder into the editor are copied in and linked as a paste is, where before dragged images were lost once the note saved
  2. Edits made just before deleting their folder are no longer lost, Command-Z after Format undoes it in one step, Clean Unused Attachments no longer removes files a subfolder note links from a parent folder, and a note with garbled text opens read-only so it is never saved over
  3. Notes deleted or renamed in Finder, git or a sync app leave the list right away, and editing the open note on iPhone no longer moves the Mac's preview or split view
  4. Titles in the note list and sidebar no longer set their last character apart, SOP showing as SO P, and folders with Trash in their name appear in the sidebar again
  5. In split view, new math, headings and local videos render as they do when the note opens, PNG export waits for diagrams to finish, switching light and dark recolours the preview in place, and dark text is softer
  6. Hovering a heading in the preview shows a link icon, a loading image shows a small turning arc, and Return after a ticked task starts an unticked one

  4. The preview's line height, heading sizes and spacing are retuned to read more easily, Chinese emphasis stays upright, and links are MiaoYan green

iPhone and iPad

  2. The reader's line height and spacing are retuned, headings on iPhone are no longer oversized, text is left-aligned, Chinese emphasis stays upright, and links are MiaoYan green
  3. A note you have open picks up edits from your Mac right away, and needless conflict prompts are gone
  4. Folders can hold one level of subfolders, shown at the top of a folder on iPhone and in an expandable sidebar on iPad, and notes can be moved into them
  5. Rename a note by editing its title, and rename or move notes from the list with a long press
  6. New icons, the MiaoYan bird on the home screen and a tidier Settings, and note cards put a shorter date ahead of the preview, with only the time for today's notes
  7. Images hold their own shape before they load, so the text no longer jumps, a slow image shows a small turning arc until it arrives, and videos show their first frame
  8. Slides written for a dark background no longer vanish, code blocks stay inside the text column, tapping the reader no longer shows or hides the toolbars, and the refresh arrows always turn when tapped
  9. Coming back from a wikilink no longer leaves the note loading, the iPad sidebar shows sync status with a refresh button, new notes use your font and size, and the Search tab's header matches the other tabs
