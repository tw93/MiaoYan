## What the 26 levels cover

They start with the everyday marks, headings, bold and lists, go on to tasks, quotes, tables and code blocks, and end with what MiaoYan draws for you, formulas and Mermaid diagrams. Each level teaches one way of writing, each chapter closes with a review that puts it together, and the last level lays out a whole note. Here is every level's Markdown, for when you forget one.

## FAQ

### When is a level done?

When your preview draws the same as the goal. The result is compared, not the text, so `*italics*` and `_italics_`, or lists started with `-` and `*`, both count; the words have to match the goal.

### Is my progress saved?

Yes. The levels you finished and what you wrote in each stay in this browser, so you can pick up where you left off. Another browser, or clearing the site's data, starts from the beginning.

### What if I get stuck?

Try the hint first. The tinted blocks in the preview are the ones that still differ from the goal. If you still cannot see it, show the answer, then start over and write it yourself.

### Does this Markdown work in MiaoYan?

Yes. The preview is MiaoYan's own, formulas and Mermaid diagrams are drawn in the app the same way, and in MiaoYan's slides `---` also starts the next slide.
